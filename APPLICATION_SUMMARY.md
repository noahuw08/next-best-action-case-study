I built and evaluated a next-best-action system for Wealthsimple’s personalized recommendations, replacing a prompt-based LLM ranker with LightGBM trained on historical client–action outcomes. I chose a trainable model so the team could benchmark, retrain, and monitor recommendation quality systematically. I validated it using historical holdouts, point-in-time feature and eligibility checks, matched comparisons against the existing LLM, and per-action statistical backtesting. On the documented offline benchmark, Precision@4 improved from 0.126 to 0.186 (+47.6% relative). I then productionized the training pipeline and scoped a contextual-bandit layer to address feedback-loop bias. The attached case study explains the results, rollout status, and remaining data and reward-design gaps.
